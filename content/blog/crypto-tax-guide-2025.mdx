## Understanding Crypto Taxes in 2025

The IRS has updated its guidance on cryptocurrency taxation for 2025, and it's more important than ever to understand your obligations. Whether you're a casual trader or a crypto professional, proper tax reporting can save you thousands.

### What's New in 2025

The IRS now requires **all cryptocurrency transactions** to be reported, including:

- Trading one crypto for another
- Selling crypto for fiat currency
- Using crypto to purchase goods or services
- Receiving crypto as payment
- Staking rewards and DeFi yields

### Common Tax Events

#### 1. Trading Crypto for Crypto

When you trade Bitcoin for Ethereum, you're actually triggering two tax events:
1. Disposing of your Bitcoin (capital gains/loss)
2. Acquiring Ethereum (new cost basis)

#### 2. Staking and DeFi Rewards

Staking rewards are taxed as **ordinary income** at the time you receive them. The fair market value becomes your cost basis.

### How to Calculate Your Crypto Taxes

```
Capital Gain/Loss = Sale Price - Cost Basis - Fees
```

**Example:**
- Buy 1 BTC at $30,000
- Sell 1 BTC at $45,000
- Trading fees: $50
- **Capital Gain**: $45,000 - $30,000 - $50 = $14,950

### Tax-Loss Harvesting

Unlike stocks, crypto is **not subject to wash sale rules** (yet). This means you can:

1. Sell crypto at a loss
2. Immediately rebuy the same crypto
3. Claim the loss on your taxes
4. Maintain your position

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Not tracking small transactions** - Even buying coffee with Bitcoin is taxable

❌ **Forgetting about airdrops** - Free tokens are taxable income

❌ **Missing exchange reports** - Centralized exchanges report to the IRS

❌ **Using FIFO when HIFO is better** - Choose the method that minimizes taxes
