A **Wash Sale** occurs when you sell a security at a loss and buy a "substantially identical" security within 30 days before or after the sale.

In the stock market, you cannot claim this loss on your taxes. But what about crypto?

## The "Loophole" Debate
Currently, cryptocurrencies are treated as "property" by the IRS, not "securities." This has led many traders to believe the Wash Sale rule doesn't apply to Bitcoin or Ethereum.

*Disclaimer: Tax laws are changing rapidly. Always consult a CPA.*

## How to Identify Wash Sales in Your Data
Even if you don't report them as wash sales, you need to track them to understand your true performance.
1. Look for a **Sell** transaction with a realized loss.
2. Check the next 30 days for a **Buy** transaction of the same asset.

## Fixing Your Report
If your tax software is flagging wash sales incorrectly (e.g., treating wrapped ETH as identical to ETH), you may need to adjust your CSV "Type" column before importing.
