## Why We Built TaxFormatter

Every tax season, millions of crypto traders face the same frustrating problem: **broken CSV files from exchanges**.

You download your transaction history, try to import it into TurboTax or Koinly, and...

❌ "Invalid date format"
❌ "Missing required columns"
❌ "Duplicate transactions detected"
❌ "Unable to parse file"

### The Old Solution: Hours of Manual Work

Before TaxFormatter, you had two options:

**Option 1**: Pay a CPA $150-300/hour to manually clean your data

**Option 2**: Spend 8+ hours learning Excel formulas and fixing errors yourself

Both options are:
- Time-consuming
- Error-prone
- Expensive
- Frustrating

### The TaxFormatter Solution: 30 Seconds

We built an AI-powered engine that:

1. **Detects** your exchange format automatically
2. **Identifies** all errors and inconsistencies
3. **Fixes** duplicates and formatting issues, and flags missing data instead of guessing
4. **Exports** to Koinly, TurboTax, CoinLedger, or ZenLedger in the format each one imports

**All in 30 seconds.**

### How It Works

#### Step 1: Upload Your CSV
Drag and drop your broken CSV file. Works with Coinbase, Binance, Kraken, and the other exchanges on our [supported list](/docs/getting-started/supported-exchanges).

#### Step 2: AI Analysis
Our engine analyzes your file and detects:
- Missing dates or amounts

#### Step 3: Instant Fix
We automatically:
- Flag missing data instead of guessing
- Remove duplicates

- Add required columns

#### Step 4: Export Anywhere
Download your clean CSV formatted for:
- Koinly
- TurboTax
- CoinLedger
- ZenLedger

### What Makes Us Different

#### 🔒 Privacy First
Your files are processed on our servers in memory, stored encrypted, and deleted within 30 days — sooner if you delete them from your dashboard. We never train on your data.

Most files parse in a few seconds; the API caps a single file at 10MB.

#### 🎯 Deterministic Parsing
Exchange detection, parsing, dates, amounts, and schema mapping are deterministic code with a fixture-backed test per verified exchange — not a model guessing. When a row can't be parsed with confidence, you get a flag, not a confident wrong number.
