# Exporting Your Data

After TaxFormatter processes your CSV, you can export it in multiple formats optimized for different tax software.

## Free Tier Export

Your formatted CSV includes:
- Normalized dates
- Standardized asset symbols
- Cleaned transaction types
- Fee tracking
- Basic cost basis

Ready to import into any tax software.

## Pro Tier Export

Everything in Free, plus:
- Flagged issues annotated in the CSV
- Cost basis adjustments
- Direct integration with tax platforms
- Multi-year reconciliation

## Supported Tax Platforms

- **TurboTax** - Direct import
- **Koinly** - Full compatibility
- **CoinLedger** - All features
- **ZenLedger** - Complete integration

Choose your platform and download the optimized format.
