# Understanding Your Results

After uploading your CSV, TaxFormatter shows you real-time **AI Insight Panels** that explain what's happening with your data.

## The Four Insight Panels

### 1. Exchange Detected ✓
Shows which exchange we recognized (Binance, Coinbase, etc.) and confirms we're using the correct parser.

### 2. Analyzing Transactions ↻
Real-time count of transactions found and being processed. Shows progress as we scan your data.

### 3. Potential Tax Issues ⚠️
Flags important tax concerns like wash sales, staking income, airdrops, and other reportable events.

### 4. Tax-Software Ready ✓
Final status showing your data is formatted and ready for export to TurboTax, Koinly, or other platforms.

## What Gets Flagged?

Common tax issues we detect:

- **Wash Sales** - Buying/selling same asset within 30 days
- **Staking Income** - Reward tokens that count as income
- **Airdrops** - Free tokens with taxable value
- **DeFi Transactions** - Complex protocols with tax implications
- **Timestamp Issues** - Dates that don't match exchange records

Each flagged issue has an explanation so you understand the tax implication.

## Your Formatted CSV

The downloaded file includes:

- Normalized dates across all transactions
- Standardized asset symbols
- Cleaned transaction types (BUY, SELL, DEPOSIT, etc.)
- Calculated gains/losses (where applicable)
- Fee tracking for deductions

Ready to import directly into your tax software.
