# Upload Your First CSV

## What You Need

- A CSV export from your cryptocurrency exchange
- Your exchange account (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, KuCoin, or Bybit)
- 2-5 minutes of your time

## How to Export from Your Exchange

### Binance

1. Log in to your Binance account
2. Go to **Account** → **Trade History** → **Spot Trading**
3. Click **Export All** (or select a date range)
4. Choose **CSV** format
5. Save the file to your computer

### Coinbase

1. Log in to Coinbase
2. Go to **Accounts** → Select your account
3. Click the **⋮ (menu)** → **Download**
4. Choose date range
5. Download as CSV

### Kraken

1. Log in to Kraken
2. Go to **Settings** → **API**
3. Create API key with "Query Ledger" permissions
4. Or navigate to **History** → **Trades**
5. Export as CSV

### KuCoin

1. Log in to KuCoin
2. Go to **Account** → **Trade History**
3. Select date range
4. Click **Export**
5. Choose CSV format

### Bybit

1. Log in to Bybit
2. Go to **Account** → **My Trades**
3. Select **Spot Trading** or **Futures**
4. Click **Export**
5. Choose CSV

## Upload to TaxFormatter

1. Visit **taxformatter.com**
2. Click or drag your CSV file into the upload area
3. Watch the **AI Insights panels** appear as we analyze your data
4. Review the flagged issues
5. Download your formatted file (named `binance_formatted_2025-12-27.csv`)

## What Happens Next

Once uploaded, TaxFormatter will:
- Detect your exchange automatically
- Count total transactions
- Flag potential tax issues (wash sales, etc.)
- Format your data for tax software compatibility
- Generate a downloadable CSV

That's it! You're ready to use your formatted data with tax software like TurboTax, Koinly, or CoinLedger.
