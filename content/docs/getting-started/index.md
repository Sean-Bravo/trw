Welcome to TaxFormatter! In this section, you'll learn how to upload your cryptocurrency CSV export and get started with formatting your tax data.

## Quick Steps

1. **Export your CSV** from your exchange (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, etc.)
2. **Upload to TaxFormatter** and watch the AI analyze your data
3. **Review the flagged issues** to understand any tax concerns
4. **Download your formatted file** ready for tax software

## What You'll Learn

- How to export CSV files from major exchanges
- File format requirements and troubleshooting
- Supported exchanges and compatibility
