# New Exchange Recommendations

**Last Updated:** 2026-01-07
**Purpose:** Identify popular exchanges to add support for

## Research Summary

Based on trading volume, user base, and community feedback, the following exchanges should be considered for future support.

---

## Priority 1: High Demand

### MEXC Global

**Why Add:**
- Ranked #2 by trading volume on CoinGecko
- 30+ million users worldwide
- 3,100+ listed cryptocurrencies
- Popular for altcoin/memecoin trading
- 0% maker fees (attractive to active traders)

**CSV Availability:** Yes - full transaction history export

**Estimated Effort:** Medium

**Key Features:**
- Spot trading
- Futures trading
- Launchpad/Kickstarter
- MX token staking

**User Pain Points:**
- Early altcoin listings = more taxable events
- High trading frequency users need automation

---

### Gate.io

**Why Add:**
- Operating since 2013 (established)
- 20+ million users
- 3,800+ tokens (most diverse selection)
- Strong in Asia and Europe markets

**CSV Availability:** Yes - comprehensive export

**Estimated Effort:** Medium

**Key Features:**
- Spot trading
- Margin trading
- Futures/perpetuals
- Structured products
- NFT marketplace
- Web3 wallet integration

**User Pain Points:**
- Complex product suite = many transaction types
- Users report difficulty with tax software integration

---

### Bitget

**Why Add:**
- 100+ million users globally
- #1 copy trading platform
- Popular with newer traders
- Growing US presence

**CSV Availability:** Yes - trade history export

**Estimated Effort:** Medium

**Key Features:**
- Spot trading
- Copy trading (unique complexity)
- Futures trading
- Launchpad
- BGB token ecosystem

**User Pain Points:**
- Copy trading creates many small transactions
- Users need automated tracking

## Deprecated/Discontinued

### FTX
**Status:** Exchange collapsed November 2022
- Listed as "Coming Soon (Q1 2026) - Post-recovery data"
- Users may need historical export for past tax years
- Bankruptcy proceedings may provide data access

**Recommendation:**
- Monitor bankruptcy proceedings
- Support historical data when available
- Low priority until data accessible

### Voyager
**Status:** Bankrupt, discontinued
- Users may have historical exports
- Consider legacy support for past data

### BlockFi
**Status:** Bankrupt
- Similar situation to Voyager
- Historical data support may be needed
