# TaxFormatter Bank Statement SEO Content Plan

## Competitive Landscape Summary

The bank statement converter space is crowded with established players (DocuClipper, BankStatementConverter.com, EasyBankConvert, Parseur, re:cap, Receipt Bot, Rocket Statements). Most focus on PDF-to-CSV/Excel conversion for accountants and bookkeepers.

**Differentiator:** TaxFormatter already has a battle-tested 6-stage data transformation pipeline. While competitors focus narrowly on PDF extraction, we position around the formatting and cleanup pain — the part where raw bank exports break when imported into QuickBooks, Xero, spreadsheets, and other tools. This mirrors the crypto tax positioning: fixing broken exports.

**SEO angle:** Target problem keywords (errors, formatting issues, import failures) rather than competing head-on for "bank statement converter." Long-tail queries have high intent and lower competition.

1. **Post #2** (QuickBooks errors) — Highest search volume, most pain-driven

### Internal Linking Structure

Every post should link to:
- Bank statement converter tool page (primary CTA)
- At least one other blog post in this cluster
- Existing crypto tax content (cross-sell)

### On-Page SEO Checklist

- [ ] H1 = post title (include primary keyword)
- [ ] H2s for each major section (include secondary keywords naturally)
- [ ] Meta title under 60 characters
- [ ] Meta description under 155 characters with primary keyword
- [ ] At least one image with descriptive alt text
- [ ] Internal link to tool page within first 300 words
- [ ] CTA at end of post + mid-post where natural
